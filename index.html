Who I Am

More than a title.
A mission.

I am a teacher, education advocate, and founder from Oyo State, Nigeria. I have spent six years standing in classrooms, school compounds, and community spaces believing that every student deserves more than what the system is currently offering them.

I graduated with a First Class in Biochemistry from Abiola Ajimobi Technical University, Ibadan, in 2025 — but long before I received that certificate, I had already started building. EduReset Initiative was founded in 2026, with no funding, no structure, and nothing but a conviction that something needed to change.

AT A GLANCE

"I am not waiting to be established before I show up. I am showing up in order to establish something."

What I Do

Three things.
One direction.

01

I Teach

Six years of teaching, tutoring, and mentoring students across secondary school levels. My classroom is wherever a student needs to be seen and challenged.

02

I Build

I founded EduReset Initiative to close the gap between what schools teach and what students need. I am also building Before the Gown — a continental programme for African university students.

03

I Lead

As Cohort President of FLDC Cohort 7 at The Fort Institute, I serve 3,800+ students across 40+ countries. Leadership for me is not a title — it is a responsibility to show up.

Where I Am Going

The work is
just beginning.

Everything built so far is the foundation. What comes next is the structure — a future where no African student has to graduate empty.

🎓

Before the Gown

A continental student initiative taking bootcamps, virtual cohorts, and campus tours to African university students — helping them Discover, Develop, and Deliver before they receive their degree.

🔗

EduConnect

A student opportunity platform connecting Nigerian and African university students to scholarships, fellowships, and professionals in their field — before the deadline passes.

🚀

EduReset at Scale

Growing EduReset Initiative into a full ecosystem — content, community, bootcamps, and a pipeline that reaches students in every corner of Nigeria and Africa.

🎙

A Voice for the Student

Using content, storytelling, and leadership platforms to amplify the voices of students who are often unseen — through the HEARD series and beyond.
